08 / FAQ

Questions, answered clearly.

AEO/GEO: visible, concise answers + FAQ structured data
What is Runable’s AI website builder?

It is a no-code website creation tool that turns a plain-language brief into a working first draft, including page structure, copy, and design. You can then edit the result and publish it from the same workspace.

Do I need coding or design experience?

No. You can begin with a prompt or template, make changes in natural language, and adjust the design directly. Code is optional, not a requirement.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. You can connect a domain you already own or search for a new one, then publish with SSL and hosting included on eligible plans.

Can I optimize my website for Google and AI answers?

Yes. The recommended workflow includes clear page titles, descriptive headings, useful answer blocks, metadata, alt text, structured data, and fast mobile-friendly pages for search and AI discovery.

Can I sell products or take bookings?

Yes. A Runable site can be shaped around commerce, bookings, services, portfolios, courses, events, lead capture, and other common business goals.

How quickly can I publish?

You can create a strong first draft in minutes. Publishing time depends on the amount of review, customization, content, and domain setup your project needs.
